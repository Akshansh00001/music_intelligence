# Executive Summary
## K-Pop Momentum Intelligence: Chart Re-Entry and Comeback Analytics

### Purpose
A dashboard to help analysts examine how songs return to a daily South Korean Top 50 playlist, how rank and popularity change after return, and how long the renewed chart presence is observed.

### What it measures
- Re-entry episodes and gaps between episodes
- Rank improvement and popularity-score change after an entry or re-entry
- Observed post-entry retention
- Descriptive comparisons by album type, album size, duration and explicit-content flag
- A transparent, exploratory comeback score and leaderboard

### How it can support decisions
Once validated real data is loaded, teams can identify tracks with repeated chart appearances, compare the shape of comeback trajectories, monitor retention after promotional events, and form hypotheses for future campaign testing.

### Guardrails
The dashboard's bundled data is synthetic and must not be used to make market claims. Re-entry and momentum are indirect signals; they do not directly measure fandom size or prove that a promotion caused a chart movement. Findings should be checked against complete daily snapshots and, where available, additional licensed streaming and campaign data.

### Recommended next steps
1. Confirm data rights, source definitions and snapshot completeness.
2. Run the dashboard on verified production data.
3. Review episode-gap and momentum-window settings with analysts.
4. Validate score usefulness against independent business outcomes.
5. Share findings with sample sizes, uncertainty and limitations alongside any recommendations.
